Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Mody Car Wash located?

add

We're at 4533 Saint Barnabas Rd, Temple Hills, MD 20748 — serving Temple Hills, Camp Springs, Oxon Hill, Suitland and the wider Prince George's County area.

Is Mody a hand car wash or an automatic machine wash?

add

Mody is a 100% hand car wash. We never use harsh automatic tunnels — every vehicle is washed by hand with soft microfiber and grit-guard buckets to keep your paint scratch-free.

How much does a car wash at Mody cost?

add

No payment is required at booking — cash is accepted on arrival. Pricing for each service is confirmed when you book or when you arrive at the shop.

Do I need to pay when I book online?

add

No payment is required at booking. Cash is accepted on arrival. Please arrive 15 minutes before your appointment.

What are your opening hours?

add

We're open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and closed on Sundays. The last appointment is accepted 30 minutes before closing.

Do you offer interior detailing and window tint removal?

add

Yes. We offer full interior detailing (deep vacuum, dashboard polish, floor mats and steam stain removal) and professional scratch-free window tint removal that restores glass to factory condition.
